Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

🔒

Fully offline

Zero cloud. Zero data collection. Even works when your Wi-Fi is off. Your conversations stay on your machine — always.

⚡

Streaming responses

Words appear as the model generates them — token by token, just like ChatGPT. No waiting for a full response.

🎤

Offline voice input

Speak and NOVA transcribes locally using Whisper. Your microphone data never touches a server.

📄

Document reading

Upload PDFs, Word docs, and text files. Ask questions about them directly in the chat.

⚙️

Deep customization

Control model, temperature, context window, themes, accent colors, and rebindable keyboard shortcuts.

🖥️

System tray

NOVA lives in your tray and is one click away. Minimize and it stays ready in the background.
